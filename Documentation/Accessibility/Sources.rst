===========================
Sources and further reading
===========================

Start with the resource that matches your task. WAI tutorials explain
practical approaches; WCAG contains the normative success criteria. Legal
applicability must be established for the project.

..  _accessibility-sources-editorial:
..  _accessibility-sources-writing:

Editorial decisions
===================

*   `Writing for Web Accessibility <https://www.w3.org/WAI/tips/writing/>`__
    introduces accessible wording, titles, headings, links and instructions.
*   `Headings <https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/page-structure/headings/>`__
    explains how heading levels communicate structure.
*   `Images Tutorial <https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/>`__
    covers informative, decorative, functional and complex images.
*   `Alternative text decision tree <https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/decision-tree/>`__
    helps determine what an image needs in its context.
*   `Images of Text <https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/images-of-text.html>`__
    explains why important text should remain adaptable.
*   `Tables Tutorial <https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/tables/>`__
    explains header cells and their relationships to data.
*   `Planning Audio and Video Media <https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/planning/>`__
    distinguishes media alternatives by content type and WCAG level.
*   `Forms Tutorial <https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/>`__
    covers labels, instructions, validation and feedback.
*   `Language of Parts <https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/language-of-parts.html>`__
    explains language marking and its exceptions.

..  _accessibility-sources-review:
..  _accessibility-sources-evaluate:

Reviewing a page
================

*   `WAI Easy Checks <https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/preliminary/>`__
    provides an initial review, with explanations of its limits.
*   `WAVE Help <https://wave.webaim.org/help>`__
    explains how to interpret WAVE's findings.
*   `WebAIM Contrast Checker <https://webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker/>`__
    checks selected foreground and background colors.

Standards and legal orientation
===============================

*   `WCAG 2.2 <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/>`__
    contains the normative requirements;
    `WCAG 2.1 <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/>`__
    remains relevant where referenced by applicable requirements.
*   `How to Meet WCAG <https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/>`__
    provides a filterable reference with supporting techniques.
*   `WAI Laws and Policies <https://www.w3.org/WAI/policies/>`__
    offers an introductory directory by jurisdiction.
*   `European Commission: Web Accessibility Directive standards <https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/web-accessibility-directive-standards-and-harmonisation>`__
    explains the relationship with EN 301 549.
*   `European Commission: European Accessibility Act <https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/policies/justice-and-fundamental-rights/disability/european-accessibility-act-eaa_en>`__
    introduces its scope and implementation.

..  _accessibility-sources-practical:

Additional practical guidance
=============================

*   `Axess Lab: Alt-texts — The Ultimate Guide <https://axesslab.com/alt-texts/>`__
    offers examples and a screen reader user's perspective on useful image
    descriptions. It is practical guidance, not a normative specification.
*   `BFIT-Bund's practical tips and guides <https://www.bfit-bund.de/DE/Downloads/Praxistipps-und-Leitfaeden/praxistipps_artikel>`__
    provide German-language guidance on topics including alternative text,
    text structure, language, contrast and captions.
*   `BFIT-Bund: Accessible documents in learning contexts <https://www.bfit-bund.de/DE/Publikation/handreichung-barrierefreie-dokumente>`__
    introduces document accessibility. Many of its recommendations also
    apply outside education.
