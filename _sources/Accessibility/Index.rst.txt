=========================
Accessibility for editors
=========================

People use websites in different ways. Some enlarge the page, navigate with
a keyboard, read with a screen reader or Braille display, or use captions
instead of listening to audio. Others need clearer explanations or more time
to understand information.

Your editorial decisions affect whether people can find, understand and use
that information. A page can have an accessible template and still contain
an unclear link, an image without a useful alternative, or a video whose
essential information is only available through sound.

..  _accessibility-what-you-can-influence:

What you can influence
======================

As an editor, you shape the content: its wording, structure, link names,
image alternatives, documents and media.

For example, meaningful headings help people find the section they need. An
appropriate image alternative communicates information to someone who cannot
see the picture. Clear instructions help people complete a form without
guessing.

Your technical team provides the templates and components that turn these
choices into an accessible page. Both contributions matter. You decide what
an image communicates; the website must output its alternative text
correctly.

The options available in TYPO3 depend on your permissions and the
configuration of your installation. If a required function is missing or the
result does not work as expected, report the affected page and element to
your technical team.

Using this chapter
==================

*   `Legal requirements <https://docs.typo3.org/permalink/t3editors:accessibility-legal>`_
    explains how standards, legal obligations and project requirements relate.
*   `Creating accessible content <https://docs.typo3.org/permalink/t3editors:accessibility-creating-accessible-content>`_
    explains everyday editorial decisions with examples.
*   `Check before publishing <https://docs.typo3.org/permalink/t3editors:accessibility-check-before-publishing>`_
    provides a practical first review and selected tools.
*   `Sources and further reading <https://docs.typo3.org/permalink/t3editors:accessibility-sources>`_
    offers guidance for questions that need more detail.

Consider accessibility when planning content, commissioning documents or
media, and updating existing pages. Including the necessary structure and
alternatives from the beginning avoids having to reconstruct them shortly
before publication.
