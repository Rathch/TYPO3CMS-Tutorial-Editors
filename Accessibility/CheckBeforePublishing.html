Check before publishing 

Review the actual page in preview, then check the published result. Backend fields alone do not show whether the website outputs everything correctly.

These checks are an editorial first review. They do not replace a full accessibility evaluation.

Review your content 

Check What to do
Title and wording Read the browser title. Does it identify this page? Check whether the main message and instructions are clear.
Headings Review the headings and levels together, including those supplied by content elements. Do they express the intended sections and subsections?
Links Follow the links. Check whether their wording matches the destination and whether the correct document version opens.
Images Compare each alternative text with the image's purpose. Check how decorative images are handled; visual appearance alone cannot reveal this.
Text in images Check whether essential poster text, dates or instructions are also available as normal page content.
Documents and media Confirm that accessible documents and necessary media alternatives are available. Play the recording and check the alternatives in the actual player.
Forms Review labels without relying on placeholders. In preview or a test environment, trigger validation errors and check whether the messages help with correction.
Presentation Enlarge the page and view it at a narrow width. Look for clipped or obscured content and information conveyed only by color.

For explanations and examples, refer to Creating accessible content.

Use tools for specific questions 

Inspect information that is not normally visible 

WAVE can reveal accessibility information and flag potential problems. For example, use it to inspect the alternative text output for an image, then decide whether that text conveys the intended information.

WAVE cannot make that editorial judgment. Its indicators need interpretation, and an absence of reported errors is not proof of accessibility. The WAVE help explains the results.

Check colors you select 

The WebAIM Contrast Checker compares foreground and background colors and reports contrast results for different text categories.

Use it when you control those colors. Text over photographs or changing backgrounds needs additional care; checking one color pair may not represent the whole image.

Learn further initial checks 

The WAI Easy Checks explain first checks for titles, headings, images, keyboard access and other aspects. Some require tools or additional knowledge. They cover only part of a complete evaluation.

Report problems you cannot resolve 

Include:

  • The page URL or TYPO3 page identifier.
  • The affected content element or component.
  • What you observed and what you expected.
  • The steps needed to reproduce the problem.

Report recurring problems so they can be addressed centrally. When updating content, also revisit its links, descriptions and media alternatives.
